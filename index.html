How to play

Every card has four features, each with three options:

  • Number: one, two or three symbols
  • Shape: circle, triangle or square
  • Colour: blue, red or green
  • Fill: solid, shaded or hollow

A trio is three cards where, for each feature, the cards are either all the same or all different. If two cards share something the third doesn't, it's not a trio.

A trio: different numbers, same shape, different colours, different fills.

Not a trio: two are red and one is blue.

Tap three cards to pick a trio. If no trio is on the table, three more cards are dealt for you.

Wrong picks cost 5 seconds and hints cost 15. In Blitz, those come off your clock.
